# Logo — closeout and restart brief
**Session:** 27 October 2026 · **Workstream:** quick, noticeable visual identity · **Status:** UNRESOLVED; no approved logo and no adopted vector source.

## Task
Find a recognizable Rock Solid Sites brand mark that works **as a circular social/profile icon**, at favicon and small responsive header sizes, as well as an optional wordmark. This needs to fit the approved site vocabulary and its bolder Treatment C schematic extension without pretending that a detailed diagram is itself a logo.

## What we explored
1. Earlier website imagery included **three overlapping circles**. That historical mark is **not** an owner-approved logo; retaining a temporary site header implementation does not confer approval.
2. A first generated round proposed four broad directions: **Signal Frame**, **Modular Grid**, **Graph Beam**, and **Cutaway Monogram**. They borrowed the geometric/structural ideas but were presented as larger brand boards and failed the owner's key requirement: they **would not work as circular logos**.
3. A second image-generation round tried four **circular RSS initials** treatments using monochrome, heavy strokes, square connection points and schematic references. These were experiments inside ChatGPT, not precise SVG/vector constructions validated against the actual tokens, nor a resolved selection.
4. The owner asked explicitly to use the **real GitHub Pages Treatment C language** rather than generic vaguely architectural imagery. No design has been selected or committed to either site/design repo.

## Strong constraints for a successful next round
- Start with a **genuine 1:1 circular crop** and the readable letters **RSS**; an attractive rectangular lockup that is poorly cropped into a circle is a failure.
- Work at the target small size *first*, then scale up, using the bolder clarity observed in the **small Treatment C booking illustration**. Avoid microtext, delicate nested construction grids and excessive ornament.
- Preserve solid/paper contrast, bold contour hierarchy and meaningful negative space. Square connection markers may appear where they contribute recognizable character; do not add arbitrary circuitry or arbitrary three circles.
- Explore meaningful variations in **letter architecture** (fully distinct RSS; joined letterforms; reversed-out cutaway; enclosed/unenclosed circle). Ensure each remains readable at actual 16/24/32/48px and in circular social crops. These are candidate tests, not approved directions.
- Ensure single-color and inverted variants survive; check visual balance and unique silhouette. Do not rely on color to distinguish R from S.
- Do not claim trademark availability or exclusive uniqueness without separate clearance research; do not copy another brand's symbol.

## What we learned
The logo is a separate creative problem from the schematic system. A schematic can contain captions and precise relationships, while a brand mark must be understood immediately, remain distinct and survive very small sizes. The **heavier compact Treatment C strokes** are useful inspiration, but diagram annotations/ports should be deliberately simplified when used as a logo. Broad image-generation boards can suggest ideas; they do not establish production vector precision, optical centering or legibility.

## Open work / next actions
1. Draw a **small controlled set** of true circle-first RSS initials **as vector marks or clean scalable icons**, not another multipanel brand board.
2. Compare them at 16, 24, 32, 48 and ~128px; test full-circle crop, light/dark reverse and recognizability without surrounding text.
3. Owner selects a promising direction, then refine letter spacing, counters, line joins, square endpoints, grid exceptions, clear space and lockup variants.
4. Obtain explicit approval before replacing any header/site/logo component, creating a design-system trademark symbol, or adding a favicon.
5. Record final mark sources, licensing/trademark boundary and usage rules separately from the MIT-licensed general illustration library.
