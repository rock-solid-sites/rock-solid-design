# Website visual foundation

**Authority:** owner-approved 25 October 2026 website design contract, adapted here to be reusable. The homepage arrangement proposed on 26 October remains under separate owner review; this repository does not silently approve its marketing copy or section order.

## Visual characteristics

- Warm off-white paper (`#FAFAF8`) and an 8px square grid using 1px black lines at 5% opacity.
- Black (`#111111`) for dark hero and footer surfaces; charcoal (`#303034`) for differentiated secondary dark sections.
- Warm stepped near-neutral cards (`#F0F0EE`, `#E8E8E6`, `#E0E0DD`, `#D8D8D5`) and structural 1px borders.
- Geist for body and headings, Geist Mono for restrained labels and annotations. Hosts must self-host or otherwise lawfully supply fonts; no font binaries are redistributed.
- 1120px max readable layout with `clamp(24px,5vw,64px)` gutters, 12-column asymmetrical sections where meaningful, 8/12px lightly eased corners.
- Purposeful linework rather than decorative dividers, restrained spacing with 8px-based rhythm, no ornamental shadows or gradients beyond functional graph-paper rules.
- Semantic headings, visible focus, keyboard and no-JavaScript access, mobile reflow, reduced motion and WCAG 2.2 AA as an engineering target (not a conformance claim).
- Do not add artificial badges, decorative index numbers, fake links, estimated reading times or fabricated showcase content to complete a layout.

## Public-private separation

These rules belong to the shared public design system. Site-specific editorial text, private customer data, operational processes, form submission logic, Cloudflare configuration, Astro page templates and actual project content belong to the consuming application. The original site logo (three overlapping circles) is historical and not an approved schematic design-system mark. No new logo has been selected.
